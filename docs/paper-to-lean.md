# Reading Conjecture 4.30 alongside the Lean statement

The numbering below is from the [authors' manuscript](https://www.mat.univie.ac.at/~bruin/papers/bcmp.pdf), not the [2017 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.07488). In the manuscript, §4.5 defines the untuned set immediately before Conjecture 4.30. Start with the [expanded consumer](../checks/VerifyStatement.lean): its `example` applies the theorem, rather than simply printing its type.

| Question for a reviewer | What the formalization says |
| --- | --- |
| **Which parameters survive the exclusion of tuning windows?** | With integer $s\ge2$ and $h=s/(s+1)$, `UT` starts from $[0,h]$ and removes each **open** interval $(T,R)$, not $(L,R)$ and not $[T,R]$. By Corollary 4.12 the pseudocenters are the positive interior finite base-$s$ points whose positive iterates under $g(x)=\{-sx\}$ avoid $(0,c)$. An iterate of zero is allowed. `Center` uses the shallowest base-$s$ grid containing $c$, not the smallest reduced denominator. See [Center, endpoints and UT](../formal/UntunedTarget.lean#L8-L31). |
| **Are we matching the original map, including its signed slopes?** | Definitions 1.1 and 1.6 concern $Q_\gamma$, not just the auxiliary fractional-part map $g$. With $\gamma$ fixed, `Trace` takes spatial limits as the displacement tends to zero from one side. The two trajectories must have both the same limiting value and the same **signed** derivative. `fiber` also requires that each side avoid the discontinuity before the meeting. Its index is $(i+1)-(j+1)=i-j$, the right time minus the left. See [original map](../formal/OriginalReturn.lean), [one-sided limits](../formal/MatchWitness.lean#L7-L12) and [matching fibers](../formal/MatchingComponents.lean#L7-L14). |
| **What stops one interval from being counted infinitely often?** | For every radius and **every finite collection** of old subsets of the real line, `Accum` must produce a point in that radius whose maximal connected component in the full matching set is outside the collection. The whole component lies in a single nonzero-index fiber. Repeatedly taking smaller intervals inside one component cannot meet this condition. The statement does not require positive *and* negative indices, or approximation from both sides. See [maximal components](../formal/MatchingComponents.lean#L21-L44) and [Accum / FullTarget](../formal/UntunedTarget.lean#L66-L72). |

## Where the universal step happens

The [final theorem](../formal/Final430.lean#L31-L38) takes just an integer `s` and `2 ≤ s`. It treats `x = 0` separately. At a positive untuned point below $h$, it invokes the **proved** [coverage theorem](../formal/Final430.lean#L8-L29), not a recurrence or density assumption on the input. Coverage rules out a gap containing the point: if such a gap existed, it would lie inside an excluded tuning window. See the [topology](../formal/GapTopology.lean), [grid center](../formal/GapGrid.lean), [pullback](../formal/GapPullback.lean) and [endpoint argument](../formal/GapFence.lean).

The [actual statement and axiom output](verification/statement-and-axioms.txt) records what the consumer saw. Lean checks deductions from the definitions. Whether those definitions express the authors' intended conjecture is a separate mathematical question; focused corrections on that correspondence are welcome.
